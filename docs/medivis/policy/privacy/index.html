PRIVACY POLICY
(GDPR COMPLIANT)

MEDIVIS Inc. (“Company”) respects your privacy and complies with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users located in the European Economic Area (EEA).

1. Data Controller

  • Company Name : MEDIVIS Inc.
  • Address : Bldg. B, Suite 908, 19 Cheongcho-ro, Deogyang-gu, Goyang-si, Gyeonggi-do, 10542, Republic of Korea (IS Biz Tower Central Phase 2)

2. Personal Data We Collect

For B2B inquiry purposes, we collect the following information:

  • Company name
  • Country
  • Business type (Manufacturer, OEM/ODM, Trading Company, Other)
  • Contact person’s name
  • Job title
  • Email address
  • Contact number

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data for the following purposes:

  • Responding to business inquiries
  • Evaluating partnership or agency opportunities
  • Business communication and follow-up

Legal Basis under GDPR (Article 6):

  • Legitimate interests (B2B communication and partnership management)
  • Consent, where required, through inquiry submission

4. Data Retention

Personal data will be retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable laws.
Once the purpose is fulfilled, the data will be securely deleted or anonymized.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

The Company does not sell or rent personal data.
Personal data may be disclosed only when:

  • Required by law or regulatory authorities
  • Necessary for internal business operations

6. International Data Transfers

Personal data may be transferred to and processed in the Republic of Korea or other jurisdictions for inquiry handling and business communications.
The Company ensures appropriate safeguards in accordance with GDPR requirements.

7. Data Security Measures

The Company implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, including:

  • Access control and internal data protection policies
  • Secure IT infrastructure and network safeguards
  • Regular employee training on data protection

8. Data Subject Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request rectification or erasure
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Request data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Requests may be submitted using the contact details below.

9. Data Protection Officer / Contact

  • Name: HYUN SUB CHUNG
  • Title: CEO
  • Email: hschung@medivis.co.kr

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy is effective as of the website launch date and may be updated to reflect legal or operational changes.
Any updates will be posted on this website.
